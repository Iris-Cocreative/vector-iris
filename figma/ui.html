Connect your Quiver API key

Vector Iris runs on Quiver's Arrow models using your own API account, so you only pay Quiver for what you make.

  1. Create an API key at Quiver and add credit (prepaid, from $10).
  2. Paste the key here.

Cost. Quiver bills your account per result. Logos and icons usually cost 1 to 10¢; detailed photos can reach about 50¢.

Privacy. Your key is kept in Figma's plugin storage on this computer and sent only to Quiver. Images you vectorize are sent to Quiver for processing.

Rights. Quiver's terms assign you the rights to what you generate; their free tier is limited to non-commercial use. Read Quiver's terms.
