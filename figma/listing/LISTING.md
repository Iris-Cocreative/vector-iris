## Description

Vector Iris turns images and ideas into clean, editable vector layers using
Quiver's Arrow models. Where a classic trace gives you hundreds of speckled
fragments, Arrow draws the shapes a designer would: on our test illustration,
17 paths and 121 anchors against Image Trace's 514 paths and 4,672 anchors.

**Three modes**
- **Trace** follows the pixels closely, gradients included. Best for logos,
  scans, flat illustration and prints.
- **Redraw** rebuilds an image from shapes and lines of its own, guided by
  your prompt. Best for photos.
- **Generate** draws new artwork from a description, optionally using your
  selection as a style reference.

Select a layer and press Vectorize: the result lands beside it at the same
size, as ordinary vector layers you can edit.

**Bring your own key.** Vector Iris is free and open source. It uses your own
Quiver API key (create one at platform.quiver.ai), so you pay Quiver directly:
in our tests $0.05 to $0.65 per image. Your key is stored only on your computer and
sent only to Quiver. There are no analytics and nothing goes to us.

Also available for Adobe Illustrator: lab.iriscocreative.com/vector-iris

Not affiliated with QuiverAI or Figma.
